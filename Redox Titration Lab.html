Redox Titration: QC Assay

Check a ferrous sulfate batch against its label by titrating iron(II) with potassium permanganate. Carry the funnel, KMnO₄ bottle, pipette, measuring cylinder and conical flasks yourself; read the burette, find the end point drop by drop, choose concordant titres, calculate and decide: release or reject.

Read the burette

KMnO₄ is too dark to see the bottom of the meniscus: read the top. Burettes are read to 2 decimal places, ending in 0 or 5.

Redox Titration Assay

MnO₄⁻ + 5Fe²⁺ + 8H⁺ → Mn²⁺ + 5Fe³⁺ + 4H₂O. Titrate 25.00 cm³ of ferrous sulfate solution (batch FS-1011, label 0.1000 mol/dm³ Fe²⁺) with 0.0200 mol/dm³ KMnO₄ in dilute sulfuric acid. KMnO₄ is its own indicator.
